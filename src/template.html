The method

How your money is handled

The rules we follow, written down so you can hold us to them.

One rate for everyone

₹4 per sq ft per month, billed every quarter. Big shop or small, nobody pays a different rate.

One account, checked twice

Collections go into the association's bank account, and every payment has been matched line by line against the bank statement.

Nobody draws a salary

No office bearer takes a fee, commission or allowance from this money. Every entry in the register is a bill paid to an outside vendor.

Spent on the building only

Housekeeping, electricity, plumbing, the water motor and lights. The four heads above cover every rupee — there is no fifth.

What is left, stays

Unspent money sits in the account for the next quarter's bills. It is not moved, lent or adjusted against anyone.

Come and check

Bills, receipts and the bank statement are open to any owner. If a figure here looks wrong, say so and it gets corrected in the open.

Figures cover 1 January to 31 July 2026. Spending of ₹54,710 up to February — January's material and light purchases, early plumbing and the February housekeeping bill — was paid in cash before the association's bank account became operational.
